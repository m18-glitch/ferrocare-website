About NACHI

NACHI-FUJIKOSHI CORP., Japan is one of the world's most respected manufacturers of High Speed Steels, Special Steels and Alloy Steels with state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities in Japan, Taiwan, Brazil, Spain and the USA.

NACHI has pioneered and patented a process of continuous heat treatment of steel bar as it is drawn. This allows the production of bars upto 2 meters length with guaranteed straightness, hardness, ovality and micro-structure. Sizes upto 2 mm are in h8 tolerance in precision bright condition.
