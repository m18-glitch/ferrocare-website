Lubricant Chemistry Management

EPT Machines

From the first day a lubricant is put into service, it begins to chemically break down — forming acids and varnish that filters cannot remove.

EPT systems use patented ion-exchange technology to remove these breakdown products continuously, managing the chemistry of turbine, compressor, hydraulic and EHC fluids — not just the particles.

Solutions by fluid

01

Rust & Oxidation Turbine Oil

Used in turbines, compressors and bearing applications. Breaks down through oxidation and forms varnish.

ICB® ROSVR® ROTMR® N₂
02

Phosphate Ester Fluid (EHC)

Fire-resistant fluids in turbine electro-hydraulic control systems. Hydrolyse and oxidise, creating acids and phenols.

ICB® FRFSVR® FRFECR® 10000TMR® N₂
03

Aeroderivative Turbine Oil

Polyol ester jet lubes that degrade from heat, water and entrained oxygen, leading to varnish and coking.

ICB® JETSVR® JETTMR® N₂
04

Anti-wear Hydraulic Oil

Used from assembly processes to steel and paper mills. Oxidation and additive depletion generate acids and varnish.

ICB® AWSVR® AWTMR® N₂

The systems

ICB

ICB®

Patented ion-exchange element that continuously removes acids, varnish precursors and dissolved breakdown products.

SVR

SVR®

Skid-mounted dialysis conditioning system that works 100% of the time, using ICB technology to remove acids and oxidation material as they form.

TMR

TMR® N₂

High-purity nitrogen generation system that removes 98% of the oxygen present and keeps water at very low levels in breathing reservoirs.

ECR

ECR® 10000

Integrated skid for EHC fluids — ICB FRF ion-exchange filters, ECR collectors, fine particulate filtration and TMR N₂ water removal in one system.
