Since 1980

Four decades of taking contamination out of oil

Ferrocare Machines Private Limited is a Pune-based manufacturer and exporter of oil purification and filtration equipment. Incorporated in 1980, the company builds electrostatic liquid cleaners, low vacuum dehydration machines, filtration systems, coolant systems and condition monitoring instruments — and exports them from India.

Who we are

An engineering company that happens to sell machines

Ferrocare was set up in Pune in 1980 to solve a specific industrial problem: hydraulic and lubricating oil that degrades long before it needs to be. The answer the company settled on — electrostatic collection — is still the technology at the centre of the range, and still the reason customers return.

Over four decades the range widened to cover the rest of that problem. Water needed vacuum dehydration. Bulk solids needed mechanical filtration. Grinding cells needed coolant systems. And proving any of it worked needed instruments. Today the company builds across all of those disciplines, so a recommendation is made against your oil rather than against a product list.

Our journey

From 1980 to today

  1. 1980

    Incorporated in Pune

    Ferrocare Machines Pvt. Ltd. set up as a manufacturer of oil purification equipment.

  2. 1980s

    Kleentek licence

    Manufacturing under licence from Kleentek of Japan; offices in Mumbai, Delhi, Daman, Bangalore.

  3. 1990s

    ELC range scales up

    Electrostatic liquid cleaners grow into a full range — 1,000+ units across India.

  4. 2000s

    Vacuum dehydration

    LVDH machines for turbine, gear and hydraulic oil; EPT Canada chemistry for phosphate esters.

  5. 2010s

    Instrumentation

    Particle counters, moisture sensors and test kits to prove the oil is clean.

  6. Today

    IoT & integrated

    MODBUS-ready controls and combined ELC + LVDH systems.

The works

Designed, built and tested in Kondhwa Budruk

A 6,000 sq ft manufacturing premises in south-east Pune. Fabrication, assembly, wiring, panel building and performance testing all happen here — which is why a duty point that falls outside the catalogue is a conversation rather than a refusal.

  • Skid, trolley and stationary formats in MS or SS
  • EPT Canada acid-control chemistry for phosphate-ester fluids
  • IoT and MODBUS control on the R-series ELC range
  • Custom flow rate, viscosity and water-load sizing per application
  • Factory acceptance testing before dispatch
